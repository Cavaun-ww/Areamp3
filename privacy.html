Privacy

This page explains what keeps about you and why.

What we store

  • Account: your email and sign-in details (handled by Supabase).
  • Profile: page name, display name, bio and photo. These are public.
  • Music: your songs (MP3 or WAV), cover art and videos. Covers are public. Full song files are stored privately: they only play in the AR camera, and the file is only delivered to people who buy, download or unlock it.
  • Seller form: legal name, PayPal email and country. Only you and the site owner (to send your payouts) can see these.
  • Purchases: what you bought, the price, the PayPal order number and payer email. Artists see their sales but not the buyer's PayPal email; buyers see their purchases.
  • Follows, likes and comments: these are public.
  • Secret locations: the exact spot an artist pins is private; listeners only see the area the artist chooses to show. When you unlock a song, your phone's location is sent once to check you're at the spot. We don't store it; we only save that you unlocked that song.
  • Messages: private between you and the other person, including photos you send. The site owner can review a conversation if it's reported.
  • Blocks and reports: private.

Payments

Card and PayPal details are entered on PayPal, never on this site, and we never see them.

Cookies

We only use what's needed to keep you signed in. No ad trackers.

Deleting your data

You can delete tracks any time from My page. To delete your whole account, email from your account's email address.
